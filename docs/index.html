Med List

One medication list the whole family can see

Med List keeps the medications, doses, allergies, conditions and doctor visits for the people you look after, and shares them with your brothers, sisters and partner so nobody has to phone around to ask.

Built for the moment it matters

  • The emergency card opens with no signal. A full copy lives on the phone. No spinner, no login wall, no “couldn't reach the server”.
  • Share it as plain text. Into a message, an email, or a printout for the doctor.
  • One list, everyone looking after them. Invite family with a code you can read out over the phone.
  • A daily check-in, if you want one. One large button. If it is not pressed by the end of the window you agreed, everyone caring for them is told.

What it is not

Med List is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. The check-in cannot tell whether anyone is unwell, and it never calls for help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
