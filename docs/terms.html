What Med List Is, and Is Not

Med List helps a family write down and share medications, doses, allergies, conditions, doctor visits and emergency contacts for the people they look after.

Med List is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and it does not give medical advice. Nothing in it replaces a licensed clinician.

Med List is not an emergency service. The daily check-in tells the caregivers in a family group that a button was not pressed. It cannot detect a fall, an illness or an emergency, it does not summon help, and it must not be relied on as a safety system. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Reminders and notifications depend on your device, your notification settings and your network. They can be late or fail to arrive. Do not use Med List as the only way anyone takes a medication.

Accuracy

Everything in the app was typed in by a person. You are responsible for what you enter and for keeping it current. Med List does not verify drug names, dosages or interactions.

Accounts & Family Groups

Med List uses Sign in with Apple. You are responsible for activity on your account.

A family group is created by one person, who can invite others. Members with the organizer or caregiver role can see and edit the whole family record. A member in the “being supported” role sees only their own.

Only add another person's health information to a family group if they know you are keeping it, or if you are the person who makes their health decisions. Anyone in the “being supported” role may leave the group at any time, and the group is told when they do.
