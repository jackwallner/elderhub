# Elderhub: Support

> Help and contact for the Elderhub family medication app by Jack Wallner.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/elderhub/support.html

## Elderhub Support

Last updated: August 8, 2026

## Getting started

On first launch, choose **Start a care record** for a parent
or partner, **I have an invitation** for an email, link or
code from family, or **Keep track of myself** for your own
private record. The guided setup shows where medications, the emergency
card, visits, readings, tasks and sharing live.

## Looking after more than one person

Open the **Care** tab and tap Add person. Mom, Dad, a
partner, or anyone else you help belongs in the same care circle. Every
helper you invite can see every person in that circle. Elderhub Plus is
required when you add the second person, but invited helpers are always
free.

## Inviting family

Sharing tab → **Invite someone**. Enter their email
address, choose whether they are a helper or the person whose record you
already built, then create the invitation. Elderhub opens an addressed
email with a tappable link and an eight-character fallback code. The code
is good for 48 hours and has no 0, O, 1, I or L, so it can also be read
over the phone. Pending invitations can be emailed again, shared another
way, or revoked from Sharing.

## Signing in

Sharing uses Sign in with Apple. It is one Face ID prompt, and there is no
password to remember. The care record itself needs no account at all.

## The emergency card

Open a person > **Add details** for allergies, conditions,
blood type and who to call, then **View emergency card** for
the one-pager. The share button sends it as plain text, so it goes into a
message, an email, or a printout. It opens with no signal.

## The daily check-in

Open a person > **Check-in**, turn it on, and agree a window
with them. They see one large button. If it is not pressed by the end of
the window, everyone looking after them is told that no check-in happened.

Elderhub cannot tell whether anyone is unwell and it does not call for
help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## Not getting notifications?

Check iOS Settings > Notifications > Elderhub, and make sure the person's
check-in is switched on in the app. Notifications depend on your network,
so they can be late.

## Subscriptions

Every feature is free for the first person. Elderhub Plus adds more people
to the care circle. One person pays and every invited member is covered.
Manage or cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on
your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Bought it and it is not showing up? Settings >
**Restore purchases**.

## Leaving or deleting

Sharing tab → leave the care circle. If you are being supported, your
family is told when you leave. Settings →
**Delete my account** removes your account; the family keeps
the record they built together.
