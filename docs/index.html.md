## What does Elderhub keep?

Medications and doses, visits, readings, tasks, notes and an emergency card. Everything the app records, it records for someone other than you.

### One tap to hand it over

Allergies, conditions, active medications and emergency contacts in
large type. Share it as plain text or read it in a waiting room. It
works with no signal.

### The list a doctor wants

Name, strength, form, purpose, prescriber, instructions, schedule
and reminders, in one list.

### Marked on someone's behalf

Log a dose as taken or skipped and the entry records who did it.
Useful when more than one person is helping.

### What the doctor said

Log a visit while it is fresh: the provider, reason, notes and
follow-up, so you do not have to reconstruct it later.

### Vitals worth watching

Blood pressure, weight, glucose, heart rate, temperature and oxygen,
recorded over time.

### The details between visits

Keep the practical details around a person in the same record, not
in another chat thread.

### One large button

Optional. If it is not pressed by the end of the agreed window,
everyone looking after them is told that no check-in happened.

### Plain text, anywhere

Share the emergency card as plain text or export the record for an
appointment. No app required at the other end.

### Everyone you look after

## More info

How it is built and why it works that way.

### The Pitch

Most medication apps assume you are tracking your own pills.
Elderhub assumes you are not.

When a family looks after someone, medications, visits, readings,
tasks, notes and contacts end up spread across a phone, a text
thread and a kitchen drawer. Elderhub keeps one current care
record per person, so the next person helping has the same answer.

### Elderhub Plus

Every feature is free for one person, including medications,
doses, reminders, visits, readings, tasks, notes, contacts,
check-in, emergency card and export. Plus adds more people to the
same care circle.

**One person pays.** The purchase covers the whole
care circle. Family members who join are never charged as separate
seats.

**Prices.** Monthly, yearly, or a one-time lifetime
purchase are available. Eligible new subscribers get a 1-week free
trial on subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app before you buy
and vary by region. Subscriptions renew automatically unless
cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and are
managed in your Apple ID settings.

### What Mattered

**Offline first.**
Every screen renders from the copy on the phone. Sync writes into
that copy and never sits in front of it, because the scenario the
app exists for is an emergency room with no bars.

**Empty sections still print.** The emergency
card and the exported one-pager print every critical heading even
when it is empty, as "ALLERGIES: not recorded". A section that
simply vanishes reads as "no allergies" to whoever is holding the
page.

**Who sees what.** A
family can work in one care circle while access follows the people
whose records are being kept. The access rules enforce those choices.

**The check-in claims nothing.** It reports that a
button was not pressed. It cannot tell whether anyone is unwell,
it does not detect falls, and it never calls for help. Nothing in
the app is allowed to imply otherwise, in the app or in a
notification.

### Not a medical device

Elderhub is an organizational tool. It does not diagnose, treat or
cure any condition, and it does not provide medical advice. Always
follow the guidance of a licensed clinician. In an emergency, call
your local emergency number.

## Frequently asked questions

## What is a good family medication tracker app?

Elderhub: Family Care Log keeps one medication list per person, with name, strength, purpose, prescriber, schedule and reminders. It is built for families looking after someone else, so doses record who marked them taken or skipped.

## Is there a free medication tracker for an elderly parent?

Yes. Every feature is free for one person, including medications, doses, reminders, visits, readings, tasks, notes, contacts, check-in, the emergency card and export. The optional Elderhub Plus upgrade adds more people to the same care circle, and invited family members are always free.

## Can family members share one medication list?

Yes, sharing is opt-in. Sign in with Apple, invite family by email or invite code, and choose which people's records each member can see.

## Is there an app with an emergency medical card for iPhone?

Yes. Elderhub's emergency card lists allergies, conditions, active medications and emergency contacts in large type. You can read it in a waiting room or share it as plain text, and it works with no signal.

## Can an app tell me if my parent has checked in today?

Elderhub has an optional daily check-in: one large button. If it is not pressed by the end of the agreed window, everyone looking after that person is told that no check-in happened. It does not detect falls, cannot tell whether anyone is unwell and never calls for help.

## Does Elderhub work without internet or an account?

Yes. It starts with a full record on your iPhone, so it works with no signal and no account. There is no advertising SDK, no behavioral analytics and no location tracking, and nothing is sold.

## Is Elderhub a medical device?

No. It is an organizational tool and does not diagnose, treat or provide medical advice. Follow your clinician's guidance, and in an emergency call your local emergency number.
