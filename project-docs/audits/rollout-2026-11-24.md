## The star prompt's good moment

Only one automatic trigger, on Today: a dose marked taken, a task marked done or a bill marked paid that leaves nothing outstanding for anyone today (every person's digest clear: no pending dose, task, appointment, low supply, bill or check-in). It was already this before the rollout; the audit of `requestReview()` call sites found only `TodayView`, with no launch, appear, tab or open-count trigger. Why this moment: the app's core action is ticking off a parent's care, and "everything is done for Mom and Dad today" is the clearest good result it has. A skipped or missed dose never counts, and an ask in front of an overdue dose in a Medical-category app is worse than none. Gates kept: 2nd cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped at the call, the write-review page ends the asks, feedback never does, never on the recipient's check-in screen (it never reaches Today), never with anything presented, and no question, pre-prompt or button in front of it. Settings keeps "Rate Elderhub" deep-linking to `?action=write-review`. No analytics capture exists in the app, so there is no `rating_asked` event.

Recommended yes: rent and care-home invoices are the bills most often due on the 31st.
