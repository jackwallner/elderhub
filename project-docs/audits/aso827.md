## Why the visible metadata stays

`Elderhub: Medication List` is the strongest search-first alternate, and `medication list` is the best pre-launch demand-to-difficulty opportunity in the research set. It is still not the right immediate title.

The current product covers:

- Multiple people in one care circle
- Medication lists, schedules, dose status, and refill work
- Visits, providers, notes, follow-ups, and appointments
- Vitals and health details
- Care tasks and family assignment
- Daily check-ins
- Emergency card and export
- Bills, contacts, search, timeline, and offline access
- Family invitations and role-scoped sharing

A medication-only title would make that system look like a generic pill app and weaken the differentiated family record. The proposed hidden field gets the exact `medication list` phrase compositionally, so the app can test that intent without sacrificing the visible family-care position.

Reconsider `Elderhub: Medication List` only after live exact-match traffic shows that medication-list discovery activates and retains materially better than broader family-care traffic.

## US keyword architecture

### Current field

```text
medication,dementia,alzheimers,memory care,pill,reminder,prescription,elder,parent,senior,medical id
```

The current field is exactly 100 bytes. It carries a coherent dementia cluster and several medication terms, but it spends bytes on `elder` and `senior`, which are broad, low-fit descriptors, and `medical id`, which can imply a system identity or emergency feature different from Elderhub's user-entered card. It also fails to include `list`, so it does not directly compose the strongest researched phrase `medication list`.

### Recommended field

```text
medication,reminder,pill,prescription,dementia,alzheimers,memory,parent,appointment,daily,list,notes
```

This is exactly 100 ASCII and UTF-8 bytes. It does not repeat `family`, `care`, `log`, `meds`, `visits`, `vitals`, or `check-in` from the visible fields.

### Wrong-intent walls

- `caregiver` results are dominated by job seekers, staffing, home-care agencies, electronic visit verification, and clock-in tools.
- `home care` and `senior care` likewise skew toward professional agencies and employment.
- `family organizer` and family/shared calendar terms lead toward household calendars rather than health and care records.

- `medical records` implies clinical records or patient-portal access. Elderhub holds information entered by a user or shared family.

Do not chase popularity that produces the wrong first launch. Qualified discovery should lead to adding the first person and first medication, not confusion about a job application, agency login, insurer, or health-system portal.

## Claim and safety boundaries

Elderhub is an organizational tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, prescribe, monitor a condition, verify adherence, contact emergency services, pay bills, book visits, or replace clinical guidance.

Safe listing claims:

- Keep user-entered medications and schedules organized for more than one person
- Record who marked a dose taken or skipped
- Save visits, follow-ups, vitals, notes, providers, tasks, contacts, and appointments
- Share a family care circle with invited members
- Open local records offline
- Create and share a user-maintained emergency card
- Let a recipient make a daily check-in that participating family can receive through the implemented sync path
- Use every feature for one person free, with more people unlocked by the actual current Elderhub Plus products

Required qualifiers:

- The emergency card is maintained by the user or family and is not an official medical record.
- A check-in is a user action, not continuous monitoring or proof of safety.
- A marked dose is a recorded status, not verified ingestion.
- Tasks record work. Elderhub does not complete calls, refills, payments, or bookings.
- Trial, renewal, lifetime, price, and family-sharing language must match current StoreKit and ASC configuration in every locale.
- Medical category declarations and App Privacy answers must reflect the actual binary and health-data sync path.
