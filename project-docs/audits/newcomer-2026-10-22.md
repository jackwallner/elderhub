# Elderhub newcomer persona audit

Date: 2026-10-22. Persona: a first-time user who does not know the app's terminology and wants one useful result immediately (here: Mom's medication on a list, with a way to tick it off).

Method: three XCUITest walks (`AgingUITests/NewcomerAuditUITests`) run on a GitHub Mac (iPhone 17, iOS 27.0, Xcode 27.1) from a wiped store with `-uitest-reset -uitest-wipe-store`. 3 passed, 0 failed. 35 attachments (31 screenshots plus control-size logs) are in `~/fleet-evidence/shift-audit-elderhub-newcomer-1-1792674527/screenshots`. Every finding marked RUNTIME was seen in those screenshots or measured in the control log; SOURCE means read from code only. Motion and frame rate were NOT measured (no video, by design for this audit).

Not walked: paywall, restore, return visit after a relaunch, Sign in with Apple completion (a headless runner cannot complete it), the join-by-invitation path. Earlier persona audits (`paudit91.md`) covered sharing and sign-out state; none of that is repeated here.

Taps to first useful result (RUNTIME): fork (1), name + permission switch + Continue (3 plus typing), Not now (1), then 4 skippable questions before the medication step (4), Add a medication (1), name + Save (2), Continue (1), 3 more steps (3), overview button (1), then Today, where the Taken button is not on screen. About 18 taps and 9 screens before the first dose can be ticked.

## Findings

### N-01 (P1, RUNTIME) The first useful control is below the fold on Today
After adding Metformin, Today opens on the "Set up Mom's record" card (3 rows plus "Show 1 more") and the 3-up "Log or look up" chip grid. The "Today's doses" section with the Taken button is not visible until the list is scrolled (`21-today-first-view.png` has no dose; `23-today-scrolled.png` has it). The control log shows `today.dose-take: MISSING` on first view. The user just entered a medication to see it here, and the screen shows a checklist telling them to add a medication-adjacent set of things instead.
Fix: when the person has at least one dose today, put "Today's doses" first and collapse the setup card to a single-line row ("2 of 6 set up, continue") beneath it.

### N-02 (P2, RUNTIME) A new medication is "overdue" the moment it is saved
The editor defaults Time 1 to 8:00 AM (`14-med-sheet-typed-keyboard.png`). Anyone adding a medication after 8 AM gets a dose already in the past: the Care tab reads "1 dose overdue" under Mom (`24-care-tab.png`) a minute after setup, with an orange warning on Today. The newcomer did nothing wrong and the first status the app reports is a missed dose.
Fix: default the first time to the next sensible slot after now (or ask "When does Mom take it?" with morning/noon/evening chips), and do not count a slot as overdue if the medication was created after that slot's time on the same day.

### N-03 (P2, RUNTIME) Name step: keyboard collisions and a truncated consent line
With the keyboard up (`03`, `04`): the floating Back capsule renders on top of the status-bar clock ("2:12" and "Back" overprint), the subtitle is clipped to "…not your own account. Y…" with no side padding, the consent line is truncated to "I have permission to keep and share th…", and the reassurance footer is hidden behind the keyboard. Without the keyboard (`02`) all of it fits, so this is a layout that was designed for the keyboard-down state. The truncated line is the one a user is asked to agree to. Continue is disabled with no explanation until the switch is on (`name.continue-keyboard-up enabled=false`).
Fix: scroll the stack under the keyboard and keep the Back button in the safe area, let the consent label wrap (`fixedSize(horizontal: false, vertical: true)`), and show a one-line hint under Continue ("Turn on the permission switch to continue") while it is disabled.

### N-04 (P2, RUNTIME) Small tap targets on every skip/back link
From the control log (points; Apple's floor is 44): sign-in "Not now" 57x18; details "Skip this step" 102x20 and "Finish without this" 136x20; "Finish later" 66x15; details "Back" is the same footnote size next to it; "Back" on the sign-in step is 79x32. These are the exits a hesitant first-time user needs most, and "Not now" is the way past an account wall (`05-signin.png`: a 52 pt black button against an 18 pt text link).
Fix: give each a `frame(minHeight: 44)` and `contentShape(Rectangle())`, as the sign-in "Use email instead" button already does.

### N-05 (P2, RUNTIME + SOURCE) Jargon before value, account before the first result
The fork asks "Start a care record / I have an invitation / Keep track of myself" and the sign-in step is headed "Create your care circle" with "An account is what lets the rest of the family see the same list you do" (`05-signin.png`). A newcomer who wants to list their mother's tablets meets "care record", "care circle" and "helper" (`19-overview.png`: "Everyone you invite as a helper can see every person in this care circle") before they have seen the app do anything. The skip exists, but the headline presents sign-in as the next required step.
Fix: lead the sign-in step with the user's own goal ("Back up Mom's list and share it with family", skip equally prominent) and use one noun ("list" or "record") plus "family" throughout onboarding; keep "care circle" for Sharing.

### N-06 (P3, RUNTIME) Eight setup questions sit between the name and the result
Date of birth, allergies, conditions, contact, medication, providers, reminders and family come first (`06` to `18`). Each is skippable and there is a "Finish later", but the medication is step 5 and the order is the emergency card's, not the newcomer's goal. The only hint of its length is "Step 1 of 8".
Fix: ask for the medication first (step 1), or after the medication step show "Mom's list is ready. Keep going (3 more) or open Today".

### N-07 (P3, RUNTIME) Empty hub shows zero stats before anything is entered
`33-person-hub.png`: "Doses today" showing a dash, "Tasks due 0", "Running low 0" above the same setup card that Today shows. Three tiles of zeros read as a dashboard that has nothing to say, and the checklist is the second copy of the one on Today.
Fix: hide the stat row until the person has a medication or task.

### N-08 (P3, RUNTIME) Sharing tab repeats its own name
`25-sharing-tab.png`: large title "Sharing" immediately above a section header "Sharing". Small, but it is the third tab and the first thing someone who tapped it for help sees.
Fix: drop the section header, or title the section "Your account".

### N-09 (P3, SOURCE, not exercised) Typed-but-unconfirmed allergy
In the details flow the `+` button is the only visual cue to commit an allergy, but Continue/Skip both call `commit(draft:)`, so text typed without tapping `+` is kept (source: `OnboardingDetailsFlow.save`). Positive: this behaves as a newcomer would hope. Not verified at runtime (the walk did not return to that step).

### N-10 (motion/keyboard, RUNTIME screenshot only, frame rate not measured here)
Opening the keyboard on the name step re-centres the whole stack and moves the clipboard icon about 300 pt up (`02` vs `03`); the layout settles late relative to the keyboard. Judge on a device, not from a still.

## What worked (RUNTIME)
- Fork copy is plain and the three options fit with no scrolling; targets are 354x93.
- Name step offers one-tap relationship chips and Continue stays in the same place.
- The medication sheet is short, Save is an obvious button, and the step confirms with "Metformin" and "Add another medication" (`15`).
- The empty emergency card is honest and has one clear "Add details" button (`40`).
- Today's Taken button is 44 pt tall and the quick-action chips say what they are for.
