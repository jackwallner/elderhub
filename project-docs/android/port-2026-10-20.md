## Result

A native Kotlin/Compose app in `android/`, package `com.jackwallner.aging`,
that works end to end as a local-only care record with the iOS paid boundary.
See `.claude/rules/android.md` for scope, parity rules and the gaps.

Verified on jmbp (evidence in `~/fleet-evidence/shift-android-elderhub-1-1792525535/`):

- 78 JVM tests (schedule engine, UUIDv5 dose ids, exporter, refills, store,
  codec, hub status lines, paywall copy, entitlements, reviewer access,
  grouped dose reminders and the rolling alarm).
  Vectors for dose ids and the daylight-saving instant come from compiling the
  iOS Swift code itself.
- 10 connected tests on agent-emu-1 (API 36): first run, adding a medication,

  persistence across launches, the disclaimer, and turning reminders on from

- `lintDebug` (0 errors), `assembleDebug`, `assembleQa` (R8). The QA build

## Bugs found and fixed this shift

- `LocalDate.ofInstant` in the export (API 34) would have crashed every
  Android 8 to 13 phone opening the emergency card share. Caught by lint.
- Onboarding relationship chips overflowed on a 1080 px phone; the permission
  switch hid under the keyboard so Continue looked dead.
- Detail screens doubled the status-bar inset.

## Finding for iOS (not changed)

`Medication.doseTimes` adds schedule minutes as elapsed time from midnight, so
on a spring-forward day an 8:00 dose is scheduled at 9:00 (and a 1:00 dose
shifts too). Android matches it so dose-log ids agree across platforms; a fix
has to land on both together.
