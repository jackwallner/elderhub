## What shipped

- Sign in with Google through Credential Manager with a hashed nonce, exchanged
  at Supabase (`grant_type=id_token`, provider `google`) for project
  `oygrxltpydcmmdtbreec`. Hidden, and the release build refused, without
  `GOOGLE_WEB_CLIENT_ID` in ignored `local.properties`.
- `GroupService`, `SyncEngine`, `SyncCoordinator`, `InviteLink` ported. Six
  tables sync both ways (people, providers, medications, dose logs, visits,
  emergency contacts); the outbox is derived from every local write, so no
  screen can forget to queue one.
- Sharing tab (sign-in, start a circle with the permission statement, members,
  invite by email/link/code, pending invitations, rename, leave or delete),
  member screen (role, who they can see, hand over, remove), join screen,
  transparency screen and a two-tab app for the person being looked after,
  conflicts screen, Settings account section (sync status, sign out, delete
  account, the not-linked-accounts note). Care keeps circle records and records
  only on this phone apart, with an explicit Share. Plus counts the circle's
  `group_billing`, and only the circle's people count toward the free slot.
- Invitation links: App Link on `https://jackwallner.com/ios/elderhub/join.html`
  and the `elderhub://invite` fallback, on a cold start and while running.
- The hub shows the server-stamped last check-in after any sync.
- Privacy policy Android section, Play data safety answers and the Play
  description rewritten for sign-in and sharing.

## Not verified, and why

- A real Google sign-in. Supabase reports the Google provider enabled, and the
  Web client id is in jmbp's `local.properties`, but no AVD here has a Google
  account and the Android OAuth clients (package plus SHA-1 for debug, upload
  and Play signing) are the separate Console job. Every sharing flow on device
  ran against the debug in-memory backend (`FakeCareServer`).
- App Link verification, which needs `assetlinks.json` at the jackwallner.com
  root with the Play app-signing SHA-256 (only known after the first upload).
- Motion at 60 fps: the AVD renders with SwiftShader (9.9 fps measured);
  native cadence is for the Air's motion task, as in the first shift.

## Decisions for Jack

1. **FCM check-in push.** Android caregivers are not told when a check-in is
   missed; they see the last check-in after a sync. Needs a Firebase project,
   a token column and FCM sending in `escalate-check-ins`.
2. **Account linking.** One person on both platforms is two accounts and two
   members. Left unlinked on purpose; linking would be Supabase manual
   identity linking plus a deliberate UI.
3. **iOS follow-ups found while porting** (not changed here): iOS omits a nil
   `deleted_at`, so a dose undone and then taken again stays deleted on the
   server; and a dirty person or medication can be overwritten by an older
   pulled copy (`applyPerson`/`applyMedication` take the server row whenever
   it is not newer).
