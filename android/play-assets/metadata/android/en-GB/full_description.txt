Most medication apps assume you are tracking your own pills. Elderhub assumes you are not.

If you help look after a parent, a spouse, or both, the real list lives in three places at once: in your head, on a scrap of paper in a kitchen drawer, and on whatever the pharmacy printed last spring. Then a nurse asks what she takes, and you are guessing.

Elderhub keeps one current care record per person, so you always have the answer.

TODAY, AT A GLANCE
Every scheduled dose for today, appointments in the coming week, and medications running low. Mark a dose taken or skipped in one tap, and the log records who did it.

THE LIST A DOCTOR ACTUALLY WANTS
Name, strength, form, what it is for, who prescribed it, which pharmacy fills it, and the instructions that matter. Times and days of the week, including once-a-week tablets. Stop a medication and it keeps its history.

AN EMERGENCY CARD THAT WORKS WITH NO SIGNAL
Allergies, conditions, every active medication, who to call first, and doctors' numbers on one page in large type. Share it as plain text into a message or an email, or read it straight off the screen in a waiting room.

WHAT THE DOCTOR SAID
Log a visit while it is fresh: the provider, the reason, the notes, and the follow-up. Add the next appointment and it shows on Today the week before.

REFILLS
Track what is left in the bottle. The count goes down as doses are marked taken, and Today tells you when it is time to refill.

SHARE IT WITH THE FAMILY
Sign in with Google and invite a brother, a sister or a partner with a link or a code. Everyone in the care circle sees the same list and the same doses, whatever phone each of them has, and a parent invited to their own record sees only that. Helpers never pay.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The record works on this phone with no account and no signal, and it is shared only with the people you invite. No ads, no tracking, and no location, ever.

ONE PERSON, EVERY FEATURE, FREE
The free version includes every feature for one person. Elderhub Plus adds more people: a second parent, a partner, or anyone else you look after.
