Elderhub is an organizational tool for tracking medications and health
information for more than one person (typically a user and their parents).
The local-only medication list needs no account and works without a network.
Family sharing is optional and uses Sign in with Apple, which is the only
sign-in method the app offers. The shared copy is
mirrored locally for offline use and syncs through Supabase when available.

Sign in with Apple: the request asks for both the name and the email scope,
and the app uses what Authentication Services returns. No screen after
signing in asks for a name or an email address. The name asked for before
sign-in belongs to the person being cared for (a parent or a spouse), not to
the account holder, and it is asked before any account exists.

Core flow:
1. Launch, choose a path, and name the person you look after
2. Optionally sign in with Apple to share the record, or tap Not now
3. Add a medication with its strength and schedule
4. Today tab lists that day's doses; tap Taken to log one
5. Care tab holds each tracked person
6. Emergency card renders a shareable plain-text summary
7. Sharing tab offers account setup and invitations by email, link, or code
8. To test Elderhub Plus, finish setup with one person, then open Care
   and tap Add person. The second-person action presents the paywall.

Every feature is free for the first person. Elderhub Plus (IAP via
RevenueCat) unlocks additional people in the same care circle. Monthly,
yearly, and lifetime purchases unlock the same capability. Invited family
members are never charged as separate seats.

The app does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and carries a
disclaimer to that effect in Settings and on the emergency card.
