The emergency card now says who to call first. The primary contact is badged on the card and marked in the copy you share or print, so whoever is holding it does not have to work it out from the order of the list.

The card is also complete. Every critical section is drawn even when it is empty, so a section that is blank reads as "not recorded" rather than as one that is missing. It prints the date of birth, the same medication detail as the shared one-pager, and when the record was last updated.

Check-in on the Everyone screen. A check-in that has not been pressed keeps that person on the day's list instead of filing them under "Nothing due today", and every person's emergency card is one tap from the screen a two-person phone opens on.

Reminders now say when iOS has turned them off. A switch that used to slide back on its own with no explanation tells you the answer is in iOS Settings, and takes you there.

Bigger controls, and a way back. The dose button and the check-in button are larger, named for VoiceOver, and follow your text size. Doses, tasks and bills can all be undone from Today.

When two phones disagree about a row, the conflicts screen shows both versions before asking which one to keep.

Signing out now clears the shared record from the phone, and a change to who can see whom applies on the next launch rather than waiting for someone to open Sharing.

Fixed: something one family member logged no longer reads as "Logged by You" on everybody else's screen.

Fixed: phone numbers group as you type, and correcting a digit in the middle of one no longer sends the cursor to the end.

Fixed: leaving setup with "Finish later" keeps the answer you were part-way through typing.
